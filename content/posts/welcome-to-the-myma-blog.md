## Why We Started This Blog

As a licensed Medicare agent, you already know the landscape changes fast. New plan options, shifting regulations, evolving client expectations — staying ahead takes more than just experience.

That's why we created the myMA blog: a dedicated space for **actionable insights** that help you enrol more clients, protect your book of business, and work smarter with technology.

## What You'll Find Here

We'll be covering topics like:

- **Enrollment best practices** — from your first client meeting to a completed application
- **Technology tips** — getting the most out of your myMA branded agent page, mobile app, and enrollment dashboard
- **Industry updates** — regulatory changes, AEP/OEP preparation, and market trends
- **Growth strategies** — building referral networks, using digital marketing, and scaling your practice

## Stay Connected

New articles will be published regularly. Bookmark this page or check back often — we're building this resource for agents like you.

If there's a topic you'd like us to cover, [get in touch](/demo.html). We'd love to hear from you.
